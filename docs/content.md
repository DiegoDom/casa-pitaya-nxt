# Content Strategy

## Primary Objective

Present Casa Pitaya as a distinctive vacation home in Puerto Vallarta and encourage visitors to contact the host or inquire about a stay.

## Secondary Objectives

- Communicate the property's capacity and characteristics.
- Highlight its location.
- Showcase the spaces and amenities.
- Explain the experience and atmosphere.
- Make contact and reservation inquiries easy.
- Set accurate expectations about the property.

## Target Audience

The website should primarily communicate with:

- Groups traveling to Puerto Vallarta.
- Families and groups looking for a complete house.
- Groups requiring accommodation for multiple guests.
- Travelers interested in staying in a residential area of Puerto Vallarta.
- Guests looking for a property with a pool and shared spaces.

> These audience definitions are strategic assumptions and should be refined if the client provides more information.

# Important Property Information

## Status

Approved as factual information

The website should clearly communicate the following characteristics when relevant to the booking decision:

- The property does not have air conditioning.
- The pool is not heated.
- There is no smoke detector.
- A carbon monoxide detector is installed.
- Stairs are required to access parts of the property.
- The pool/jacuzzi area does not have a safety fence or lockable gate.
- There are no exterior security cameras.
- There is no washing machine.
- There is no dryer.
- There is no heating.

> Safety and limitation information should not be hidden when it is relevant to guests.

# Content Rules

## Must Do

- Use factual information from `docs/client.md`.
- Keep the tone warm and welcoming.
- Communicate the property's personality.
- Be transparent about limitations.
- Prefer concise copy.
- Use real photography whenever possible.
- Keep important practical information easy to find.

## Must Not Do

Never invent:

- Prices
- Availability
- Reviews
- Testimonials
- Awards
- Certifications
- Amenities
- Services
- Distances
- Guest experiences
- Policies
- Property features

Do not claim:

- The property is beachfront.
- The property is a luxury property.
- The property has air conditioning.
- The pool is heated.
- The property has a washing machine.
- The property has a dryer.
- The property has a smoke detector.
- The property has exterior security cameras.

unless these facts are explicitly confirmed or the existing client information is updated.
